Analyses de films — film analyses

This site publishes sourced critical syntheses of films. It keeps its French name in English as everywhere else: a name is glossed once, not translated.

Here, artificial intelligence is not an autonomous colleague but a set of capabilities acting under an explicit human mandate — never on its own initiative — in the service of a collective intelligence whose decisions remain, at every step, those of Christo Datso.

Three pages, and what each is for

  • The manifesto — what the site commits to, signed by its author.
  • Who we are — who writes here, with which instruments, and what the writing does not claim to be.
  • How this site works — the mechanism itself: how a text travels from an idea to a published page, what gets checked, and what sometimes fails.

The analyses are in French

Every analysis on this site is written in French, and none of them is translated. What exists in English is the apparatus around them: what the site holds to, who writes it, how a page gets published. The analyses are the work; these pages are the terms on which the work is done.

The full index stays where it is, and stays French: browse the corpus (in French).
